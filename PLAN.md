The walkthrough revision supersedes the prior visible six-stage, exact-two-tool, single-case Demo
UX. It does not reinterpret historical evidence or weaken the one-selected-case, one-admitted-call
live safety boundary. Existing Contract v1/v2, Result v2, regression v1, sessions, storage keys,
toolset identity, receipts, and frozen artifacts remain parseable and immutable. New behavior is
implemented through additive versioned schemas and identities.

## Sequential milestones

1. **R0 — Authority, evidence, and predecessor reconciliation: PASS**
   - Verify the master contract, all sources and hashes, current official requirements, private
     workspace authority, production/CI predecessor, frozen evidence, and additive compatibility
     strategy before implementation.
2. **R1 — Real template library and strict contract foundation: PASS**
   - Add an immutable registry for the real reference-checkout tools, a strict versioned 2–4 tool
     Demo catalog, a bounded 0–6 case suite, selected-case live Contract v3, and isolated tab-scoped
     storage with canonical identities and fail-closed parsing.
3. **R2 — Judge entry and Stage 1**
   - Make the exact built-in-Browser entry command explicit, teach the owner/agent/trusted-state
     boundary, make the synthetic cart safely editable and resettable, and keep the owner document
     free of registered target tools.
4. **R3 — Stage 2 real WebMCP catalog**
   - Let the judge choose two to four existing real tools, edit only agent-visible metadata, inspect
     fixed schemas/handlers/effects, reset defaults, and preview the exact discoverable catalog.
5. **R4 — Stage 3 suite builder and nested arm review**
   - Let the judge build, edit, remove, select, and validate up to six successive contract cases;
     show the suite as it grows; nest final selected-case review and arming inside this stage.
6. **R5 — Fresh-agent handoff and explicit live-observation boundary**
   - Keep owner preparation, fresh-context receipt, readiness, registration, and observation
     distinct. Opening the opaque URL—especially in ordinary Chrome—must not register tools or
     start the timer. Only an explicit start may expose the exact frozen catalog and admit one call.
7. **R6 — Verdict, diagnosis, regression, and deeper proof**
   - Present requested/required/observed behavior, trusted before/after state, ledger diff,
     assertions, deterministic diagnosis, recommended next step, honest evidence tier, save/rerun
     behavior, a controlled mismatch example, and links to the five-tool Lab plus reference results.
8. **R7 — Collateral, independent closeout, and exact-SHA release**
   - Update judge-facing documentation and capture plan, run complete tests/build/CI and targeted
     security/privacy/licensing/publication/accessibility reviews, preserve frozen evidence, deploy
     the exact private successor, and verify Production natively without provider/model calls.
9. **R8 — Authentic fresh-agent and final human handoff**
   - Prepare one consolidated sequence for the genuine built-in-Browser observation and video, then
     stop at Sergio-only legal/claims/video-publication/public-repository/tag/Devpost/freeze gates.

No milestone advances until its acceptance proof is green. No arbitrary-tool/site builder,
generalized scanner, provider call, public release, tag, or Devpost mutation is authorized by this
implementation plan.
