2:40–2:50, and the encoded upload must remain strictly below three minutes. This document does not
attest that capture or publication happened; the final media manifest and submission receipt are

## Recording and edit settings

- Timeline: target exactly 2:49. An accepted final may be 2:40–2:50 and must remain below 3:00
  after encoding and YouTube processing. Do not add an Invocation Integrity tail after 2:49.
- Canvas and export: 1920×1080, 16:9, 30 fps constant frame rate, SDR Rec.709/sRGB, H.264 High
  Profile at 12–20 Mb/s, and AAC-LC 48 kHz at 192 kb/s or higher.
- Browser: use the exact supported release-test Chrome/WebMCP build at 100% page zoom, one clean
  window, and one Thurstone tab except while displaying the already-retained Direct clip. Hide the
  bookmarks bar, unrelated extensions, notifications, password-manager overlays, personal tabs,
  email, account menus, and provider consoles.
- Capture: keep the cursor visible without a click halo that hides text. Record live calls
  continuously at normal speed. Do not fabricate terminal output, conceal errors with zoom, use
  motion interpolation, or place a freeze frame over a running status.
- Audio: Sergio's original English narration, close microphone, peaks below −3 dBFS, integrated
  loudness near −16 LUFS, noise floor below −50 dBFS, no clipping, no music, no synthetic voice, and
  no sound effects.
- Edit: use hard cuts or 4–6-frame dissolves only. Remove waiting or scrolling only between settled
  states. Preserve at least 1.5 seconds after each result or limitation becomes readable. Retain lane
  labels for the full shot.
- Captions: upload the reviewed English WebVTT track and burn in only lane/safety labels. Verify
  selectable captions after YouTube processing.
- Master files: retain the source recordings, retained Direct clip, edit project, caption file,
  final MP4, final thumbnail, and an SHA-256 media manifest outside the repository until the release
  process records the approved public URL.

## Retake checklist

Retake the affected live shot—or re-edit only the same retained Direct source—if any item is true:

- the final duration falls outside 2:40–2:50, exceeds 2:50 after processing, or differs materially
  from the 2:49 timing plan;
- the URL, release identity, secure context, exact initial catalog, readiness, or seeded fixture is
  missing or wrong;
- the Direct segment is anything other than retained <code>direct_review_a</code>, hides its actual
  outcome or working-name context, or resulted from a fresh prompt/model attempt;
- a native call is retried, duplicated, canceled, stale, replayed unintentionally, or lacks one
  handler trace and its observable effect;
- <code>cart_update</code> does not visibly show Stoneware mug 2 → 3, state revision 0 → 1, and
  exactly one native call;
- reset is not verified before the judge shot, or the judge shot performs provider inference instead
  of archived replay;
- a spinner, error, toast, console warning, failed request, unsupported-runtime notice, or personal
  notification appears;
- a cut crosses a call start/result boundary or makes retained evidence look live;
- the semantic result is anything other than 23/24 → 23/24, the residual row is not Fail → Fail, or
  the script implies improvement;
- Invocation Integrity does not show exactly II-01/II-02/II-03, 3/3, four native calls, 28
  assertions, zero model calls, trusted server state, and a separate denominator;
- the one-line description diff, human freeze, separate lanes, simulation notice, no-endorsement
  language, one-trial limitation, or Invocation Integrity testing-only limitation is absent;
- captions disagree with the narration/table/timeline, cover evidence, exceed two visible lines,
  contain a product/tool typo, or drift by more than 250 ms;
- narration clips, peaks above −3 dBFS, or is hard to understand at laptop volume;
- any key, cookie, capability, authorization identifier, private path, account identity, unrelated
  tab, or personal data appears.
