- Never present native diagnostic buttons as model selection. Never merge Custom Probe, Direct Site Tools, native-plumbing, or judge evidence into one denominator.
- Use the lane labels in the shot list without abbreviation. They are part of the truth boundary.

## Recording and edit settings

- Canvas and export: `1920×1080`, 16:9, 30 fps constant frame rate, SDR Rec.709/sRGB, H.264 High Profile, target 12–20 Mb/s, AAC-LC 48 kHz at 192 kb/s or higher, final duration `2:45–2:49` and strictly below `3:00`.
- Browser: the exact supported release-test Chrome/WebMCP build at 100% page zoom; one clean window, one Thurstone tab except during the Direct Site Tools shot, bookmarks bar hidden, unrelated extensions and notifications hidden, no password manager overlays, no personal tabs, email, account menu, or API/provider console.
- Capture: cursor visible with no click halo that hides text. Record each call continuously at normal speed. Do not use fabricated terminal output, zoom-based concealment, motion interpolation, or a freeze frame over a running status.
- Audio: Sergio's original English narration, close microphone, peaks below `-3 dBFS`, integrated loudness near `-16 LUFS`, noise floor below `-50 dBFS`, no clipping, no music, no synthetic voice, and no sound effects.
- Edit: hard cuts or 4–6-frame dissolves only. Waiting/scroll removal is allowed only between settled states. Preserve at least 1.5 seconds after every result or limitation becomes readable. Use the lane labels exactly and retain them for the full shot.
- Captions: upload the reviewed English WebVTT track and also burn in only the lane/safety labels. Do not burn the full transcript if it would obscure evidence. Verify captions in YouTube after processing.
- Master files: retain the lossless or high-bitrate source, edit project, caption file, final MP4, thumbnail PNG, and an SHA-256 manifest outside the repository until the release process records the approved public URL.

## Retake checklist

Retake the affected shot—never cover the problem in editing—if any item is true:

- the URL, build identity, secure context, exact initial catalog, readiness, or seeded fixture is missing or wrong;
- a second Direct semantic attempt is made, the usable outcome is hidden, or a non-`order_review` result is paired with the primary narration instead of the declared contingency;
- a native call is retried, duplicated, canceled, stale, replayed unintentionally, or lacks one handler trace and its observable effect;
- `cart_update` does not visibly show Stoneware mug `2 → 3`, state revision `0 → 1`, and exactly one native call;
- reset is not verified before the judge shot, or the judge shot performs a new provider inference instead of archived replay;
- a spinner, error, toast, console warning, failed request, unsupported-runtime notice, or personal notification appears;
- a cut crosses a call start/result boundary or makes pre-recorded evidence look live;
- the baseline/revised result is anything other than `23/24 → 23/24`, the residual row is not Fail → Fail, or the script implies improvement;
- the exact one-line description diff, human freeze, separate namespaces, simulation notice, no-endorsement language, or one-trial limitation is absent;
- captions disagree with narration, cover evidence, exceed two lines, contain a typo in a product/tool name, or drift by more than 250 ms;
- narration clips, peaks above `-3 dBFS`, is hard to understand at laptop volume, or the final duration falls outside `2:45–2:49`;
- any key, cookie, capability, authorization identifier, private path, account identity, unrelated tab, or personal data appears.
