It distinguishes source verification from implementation proof: **source-confirmed** means the cited official text was checked, while **evidence-confirmed** means a retained artifact or deterministic verifier currently proves the ToolProof response. Evidence confirmation is not the same as public release, legal assent, organizer acceptance, or submission.

The status cells in the rule tables preserve the original 2026-08-26 planning baseline. The dated reconciliation below is the controlling **pre-deployment evidence-freeze snapshot** and supersedes those legacy cells where they differ; it is intentionally not a mutable post-release dashboard. Status vocabulary: `evidence-confirmed`, `source-confirmed`, `resolved`, `pending Gate 7`, `Gate 8 artifact prepared`, `pending Gate 8`, `pending Gate 9`, `human Gate 9`, and `not applicable`.
